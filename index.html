Reading the panels

Shadow. The darkness slider sets how much sunlight it blocks.
Bypass diodes. Three per panel, one for each section of two cell columns. A diode lights up when it carries the current around its section.
Bypassed section. Its power is lost, but current keeps flowing, so the rest of the string keeps working.
−1.3
Panel voltage where the inverter runs. A small negative value means the whole panel is bypassed.

How the numbers are made. 10 panels in series, mounted upright. Each panel is rated 50 V open circuit, 15 A short circuit and 600 W (a fill factor of 0.80 is assumed), with three bypass diodes. Both panel types share the same unshaded ratings, so every difference comes from how the cells are wired. The inverter is assumed to find the string’s highest power point. Standard cells in full sun (1000 W/m², 25 °C); cell-level shading features such as the Hi-MO X10’s anti-shading design are not modeled. An illustrative model, not a product datasheet.
